Who is Mdihni Advisory intended for?+

The focus is on entrepreneurs, smaller organizations and project teams looking for clearer financial information, structured analysis and stronger management insight.

Can I discuss a specific financial project?+

Yes. Send a short outline of the question, the information available and your desired outcome. We can then determine whether it fits the current scope and availability.

Does Mdihni Advisory provide statutory audits or regulated advice?+

No such services are advertised or offered through this website. For work requiring a statutory auditor, licensed financial adviser or specialized tax or legal professional, consult an appropriately qualified provider.
