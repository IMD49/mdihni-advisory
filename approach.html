A DISCIPLINED PROCESS

From context
to clarity.

Good analysis starts with knowing what a decision needs, not with building the biggest spreadsheet.

01
DISCOVER

Understand the question.

Clarify the objective, intended audience, context and information available. Start by defining what a useful outcome looks like.

02
FRAME

Build the right structure.

Organize financial information, set boundaries and choose appropriate indicators, assumptions and methods.

03
EXAMINE

Analyze the evidence.

Identify relationships, possible drivers, variances and risks. Distinguish reliable findings from assumptions and uncertainty.

04
COMMUNICATE

Make the insight useful.

Present findings clearly and connect observations to the practical business decisions they may inform.

05
REFINE

Improve with feedback.

Where appropriate, refine the analysis as new information or business questions emerge.

THE PRINCIPLE
“Clarity is not about having more information. It’s about understanding the information that matters.”
