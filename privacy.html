Privacy notice.

How personal information is handled when you visit this website or contact Mdihni Advisory.

UPDATED12 October 2026

This notice describes the website, email contact and the hosting used.

1. Who this notice concerns

This notice applies to the public website at mdihni.com. Questions about the handling of your personal information can be sent to contact@mdihni.com.

2. What we collect

This is a static website. The enquiry form prepares a message locally in your browser and opens your own email application, or lets you copy the message. The form does not send or store submissions. If you email us, the information you provide, such as your name, email address and message, is received through our STRATO email service.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting and email providers may process technical data to deliver and secure their services. Further information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Why the information is used

Information sent by email is used to read, respond to and manage business enquiries, and where appropriate to take steps requested before any agreement. Depending on the circumstances, processing may rely on legitimate interests or steps prior to entering an agreement.

4. Cookies and tracking

The website code does not set cookies, use browser storage or include external analytics, advertising trackers or tracking pixels. Fonts, images and scripts are hosted locally. This notice will be updated if the services or integrations change.

5. Storage and disclosure

Messages may be stored in the business email account for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the correspondence or satisfy applicable record-keeping duties. We do not sell enquiry information. Technical service providers may process data as part of operating email or website hosting.

6. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or other appropriate handling of your personal data. Contact contact@mdihni.com. Individuals in the Netherlands may also contact the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

7. Updates

This notice may change if the site adds services or the way information is processed changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
